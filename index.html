Haptics Prototyping

Vibrational Feedback cuffs for Dancers with Hearing Impairment

Physical prototype of vibrational feedback cuffs for dancers with hearing impairment, developed in a group for "Prototying in Interaction Design" during my ERASMUS semester at Chalmers Insititute of Technology in Gothenburg, Sweden. LED stripes were attached to the outside of the cuffs and a vibrational motor on the inside, providing both visual and haptic feedback. These elements were attached to a microcontroller, which was programmed to provide feedback based on the music's beat and rhythm. Focused on microcontrolling and physical prototyping.

Microcontrolling · Prototyping Sept – Nov 2024
